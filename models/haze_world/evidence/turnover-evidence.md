# Turnover: empirical context and model interpretation

The identity and abstract of Hoehler & Jørgensen (2013), *Microbial life
under extreme energy limitation*, *Nature Reviews Microbiology* 11, 83–94,
were checked against the [publisher](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrmicro2939)
and [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23321532/).
DOI: 10.1038/nrmicro2939.

Their review describes biomass replacement in energy-limited subsurface
communities over centuries to millennia, much slower than favorable culture
conditions. It also emphasizes energy fluxes below typical culture-derived
maintenance estimates. This supports exploring slower physiology; it does
not calibrate a particular Archean community.

In our equations, `L` removes active biomass in proportion to its stock.
Identifying a measured biomass-replacement time with `1/L` requires an
additional model assumption; it is not a direct measurement of cell death
or dormant-cell survival. T1–T3 vary the declared loss parameters and retain
the existing uptake and maintenance laws. The resulting regime map is a
conditional model result, not an estimate of ancient Earth mortality rates.
