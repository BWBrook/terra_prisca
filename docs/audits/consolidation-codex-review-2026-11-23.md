The shorter abstract deliberately leaves the original 36-state inventory to §3.2, the detailed prices and habitat widths to §3.4, and the imposed-warming results to §3.5. Those results and their units are still there.

**Suggested wording** following the three unchanged opening sentences, incorporating Gemini’s assembly edit. The complete abstract is 196 words by whitespace count.

> Temperature shifts the competitive advantage, so their competition can hold temperature exactly constant as hydrogen supply changes. This needs some diverted hydrogen to stay out of methane for good, through oxidation, export or burial. At the reference supply the equilibrium loses local stability at a climate response time of 0.15 years without acetate recycling, or about one year with half recycled. With full acetate recycling and biomass export, sampled margins reach 21–31 years inside a narrow window of lower supplies. On four tested branches with acetate feedback or differing habitat temperatures, a 30-year margin costs a local sensitivity of a fraction of a kelvin per e-fold of supply. Some habitat mixtures tolerate centuries at 1–2 K per e-fold. All 24 closed, uniform reference invasions end with the invader alone. With fixed products, exclusion takes decades. Refuges or immigration can sustain the losing group until the climate catches up and the mixture forms.
