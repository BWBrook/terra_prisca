# Review of the invasion repair

Codex, 9 November 2026. Reviewed `cb194fb` and its regenerated results.

The invasion repair, occupied-branch/null fidelity reporting and revised
Fig. 1a interpretation address the first review. The committed sweep diff
changes only the Fig. 1d downward point at L = 1.224. The original paper's
comparisons are unchanged. The 14 tests present in that commit pass.

An independent check found one return-value edge case: on the fifth and final
retry, reseeding could change the returned population while leaving
`converged=True` from the preceding state. For neutral daisies at
L = 1.1439288525058846 with `extinct_below=0.01`, the positive equilibrium is
below the cutoff and each retry reseeds it. The previous routine returned an
unsettled seed with a convergence flag.

This turn sets the flag to false whenever reseeding changes the state and
adds an exhaustion regression. It does not change the physical equations or
the existing 1983 sweep results. There is no evidence that the default
0.002-grid reconstruction reaches the retry cap. The remaining numerical
limit is now reported truthfully rather than hidden by the flag.

Verification after that change: all 28 repository tests pass (15 Daisyworld,
13 response-data/interface checks). Regenerating the Daisyworld sweeps and
scan fidelity tables gives byte-identical CSVs to `cb194fb`, with every
default sweep step reporting convergence.

The Daisyworld reconstruction can serve as the current baseline. Fig. 1a's
historical cause remains unresolved, and the nearest-ink score retains its
documented limits; neither needs another review cycle before starting the
haze-response work.
