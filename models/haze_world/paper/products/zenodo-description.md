AI contributions. This work comes from a research group of one human and several AI systems. Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic), with Sonnet 5.5 for the display items and supplement, wrote the specifications, reviewed each stage and drafted the paper. Codex GPT-6 (OpenAI) built and ran the coupled population–climate simulations, independently checked the analyses, reviewed each manuscript stage and corrected the figures and supplement. Gemini 4 Carbon (Google) reviewed the work independently. Claude Fable 5.2 (Anthropic) advised, posed five pressure tests and drafted the abstract. GPT-6 Astra Pro (OpenAI) gave an early independent review. Barry W. Brook conceived the question, supervised, decided and reviewed, and is responsible for this record. The code, analyses, figures and text were generated by these systems under his supervision; the correspondence record and claims ledger in the deposit document each step.
