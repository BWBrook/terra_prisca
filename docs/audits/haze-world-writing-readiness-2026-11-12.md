## The scope on which I concur

The paper asks when microbial hydrogen competition can produce climate
regulation in this model, how climate-response inertia changes its stability,
what the specified graded mechanisms cost in residual sensitivity, and how
finite assembly depends on history and persistence mechanisms. It does not
reconstruct an identified Archean biosphere or establish a universal control
law.

The contribution must name the particular climate-delay, graded-response and
finite-population experiments. Seto & Akagi already analyse population ODEs,
nonlinear time courses, trajectories and local stability. Therefore neither
"dynamics are new" nor an unqualified "assembly was not studied" is an
appropriate novelty claim. Their sign-based root result also has an E2=0
simplification. These are writing corrections, not new experiments.

Likewise, use parametrisation/application rather than ecological calibration.
The chosen capacity transfer admits the reference crossing; the dry-mass
alternative displaces it off the cooling branch. The scan does not establish
that only one precise capacity normalisation can ever support a crossing.

### Integral action and the leak analogy

For the positive homogeneous populations define z=ln(X_A/X_M). Their
equations give the exact identity

    z' = g_A(H,T,C) - g_M(H,T,r).

This integrates the fitness difference. It is not automatically a global
controller integrating only the temperature error. Common mortality cancels
directly from the difference, even though it changes the equilibrium resource
requirements. With acetate feedback, the fitness signal depends on an
additional product stock. In habitats with dispersal, the local ratio also
has the state-dependent term

    d [mean(X_A)/X_Aj - mean(X_M)/X_Mj].

Thus "effective integral action near coexistence" and "graded or leaky-control
interpretation" are defensible framing. A literal equivalence to a specified
single leaky integrator requires its reduction to be shown during writing.
The calculations establish graded response and delay margins without that
equivalence. They do not identify an antithetic molecular controller.

The primary papers by [Yi et al. (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.97.9.4649)
and [Olsman et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2019.06.001)
already establish biological integral-control and performance-trade-off
precedents in their particular architectures. Targeted primary text was
checked for this review. No claim of full reading of the entire control-theory
list, or transfer of their detailed theorems to this ecosystem, is made.
Keep Claude's full-reading flags until those citations are developed.

### Methane diversion is the condition

The full-budget evidence already makes a stricter phrase in the proposed
framing unnecessary: a non-methane *acetate* fate is not mathematically
necessary for every positive-coexistence interval in the full model. The
static audit has eta_A=0.894871 and eta_M=0.941322 after assimilation. Even
at f=1 its nominal-supply interval is 0.229178–0.239907, a ratio of 1.04682.
This is a positive-coexistence interval, not a new claim of stable regulation
through realistic delays.

State the condition as differential methane yield and material retained
outside methane production, including the model's assimilation/export. The
reference f=0/.5 runs use an explicit non-methane acetate route; large
regulation ranges depend strongly on that route. Complete recycling sharply
narrows the range. Do not claim that the full model's range is exactly 1/f,
or that the f=1 range is exactly zero. This qualification uses the existing
audit and needs no new run.

### Keep the demonstrated limits visible

- The 0.07–0.32 K per e-fold result is a local-derivative cost on four specified
  branches for a thirty-year first-loss margin. It is not a universal minimum,
  a finite forcing response or an empirically calibrated precision.
- Near-zero continuity is local; censored positive-grading transitions do not
  prove global smoothness. Later restabilisation can occur.
- The proposed power-law slope band and general sub-century weak-mode formula
  have counterexamples. Preserve them in the paper.
- Report C59's lag changes quantitatively rather than calling a roughly 20%
  relative change negligible. Its derivative holds nominal donor forcing and
  the external reservoir definition fixed; a smaller slope is not by itself
  a stronger regulator.
- Invasion-order endpoints are results of the stated single-habitat finite
  rule. Refuges or dormant cells can alter them; uncensored later subcell
  minima do not establish joint finite extinction or inevitable planetary
  history dependence.

These are the bounds of the paper we can now write, not additional gates to
starting it. I accept the final triage as closed or deliberately narrowed at
